The finite Cartan matrix classification is adapted for Lean 4.28.0 and the
repository's pinned mathlib revision from [mathlib4 PR 45800, commit
3493731](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/tree/349373162060fbe4fa4b21accb95142a3337dc6d/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Cartan).
The individual source headers credit the original authors. The adapted
mathlib files are distributed under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE).
The port uses the pinned definitions of the classical and exceptional
Cartan matrices, and adapts imports, tactics and lemmas to that revision.
