# Production review

- Full twelve-minute storyboard and a 1,433-word first narration draft, with recording directions.
- Separate 78-second silent pilot, rendered in Hyperframes at 1920×1080, 30 fps.
- A worked F₄ row before the six-row comparison, with each coefficient attached to its own simple factor.
- Explicit semisimple mixed-term bridge, separate background-global status for SU(3)ₘ, and the manuscript's second-classification boundary.
- Both coefficient ports remain visible at the E₆ result. The E₈ projection uses all 240 actual roots and is labelled as a projection.

- Editable captions, source manifest and export-check script.

## Independent editorial passes

Two agents independently reviewed the paper and existing film, then reviewed the new storyboard and narration. One concentrated on physics and the theorem's logical boundaries; the other on prerequisite order and visual comprehension. Their findings were integrated rather than converted into a numerical quality score.

The pedagogical suggestion to illustrate the pair-first operation was accepted. An imprecise suggestion to compare unreduced pairwise groups before taking the derived group was not: the plan explicitly forms the derived groups before comparing their dimensions, matching the paper.

These were agent editorial reviews. No human novice viewing study has yet been run, and no claim of matching a named creator's finished production quality is made.

## Checks and their scope

Eight local tests pass in the authoring workspace. They cover displayed coefficient agreement, independent SU(N) coefficient arithmetic, the single zero-containing row, root count and lengths, projection orthonormality, representation dimensions, cue coverage and absence of autonomous time/randomness. The website-comparison test skips explicitly when that sibling project is absent; the other seven are standalone.

Hyperframes static lint, runtime, sampled layout and sampled text contrast checks passed after correcting the overlap annotation for KaTeX's compound not-equal glyph. Only the slash glyph is exempted from collision checking; no scene or general formula region is exempted. The tool's separate motion-analysis pass was disabled, so its reported “ok” is not treated as evidence of motion validation.

Decoded video frames were inspected separately from browser snapshots. The browser snapshot renderer and native video renderer can select different text fonts; both were checked for legibility. Cross-machine pixel identity is not asserted. The export verifier checks codec, resolution, frame rate, duration, intentional absence of audio and diversity of decoded sampled frames. Frame diversity is not a substitute for watching the final cut.
