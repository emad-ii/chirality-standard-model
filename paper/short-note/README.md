# Original note · April 2026

[*The Gauge Algebra of Chirality*](paper-iii.pdf) is Emad Mostaque’s original two-page note from April 2026, preceding the longer September paper. [LaTeX source](paper-iii.tex).

The note asks a distinct classification question: the ambient compact algebra is simple, and its maximal subalgebra has exactly two nonabelian simple factors. It tests both ordered factor choices and the allowed orientations of complete joint irreducible constituents.

The longer paper starts instead from effective compact inclusions whose entire adjoint complement has complex type and nonzero cubic radical. Its six-candidate reduction and the original note’s five exceptional rows belong to these different domains.
