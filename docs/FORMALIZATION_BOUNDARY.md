# Proof Allocation and Trust Model

## The split

This project deliberately has three mathematical authority layers and one interpretive layer.

1. **Finite formal layer.** Lean reconstructs the exceptional full-rank inner-toral census and its Cartan trace-cubic selector, then checks the concrete $E_6$ root/reflection data and incidence calculation.
2. **Executable audit layer.** Exact Python programs reproduce the finite searches and arithmetic used by the manuscript, often through independent realizations.
3. **Manuscript proof layer.** The paper proves the compact-Lie,
   completeness, invariant-theoretic, infinite-family, and
   representation-theoretic bridges around the finite proof leaves.
4. **Physical interpretation layer.** The complete-matter assumption (M) is named and remains conditional.

These layers are complementary, and each carries its stated mathematical authority.

## Lean finite theorem surface

The exceptional census in
[`CubicAnomaly/Classification/Exceptional.lean`](../CubicAnomaly/Classification/Exceptional.lean)
starts directly from five authored
Cartan matrices for $G_2$, $F_4$, $E_6$, $E_7$, and $E_8$, together with their
integer symmetrizers. Lean then:

- regenerates all five root systems by reflection closure;
- exhausts every root-lattice functional modulo two and three, modulo the
  Weyl action generated by the simple reflections;
- retains the full-rank order-three rows with equal conjugate-grade sizes and
  a connected grade-one root graph, recovering exactly the six numerical
  component signatures;
- checks the connected odd-root graph for every full-rank inner involutive
  row used by the complex-type exclusion;
- evaluates the exact grade-one trace cubic on coroot spanning sets and finds
  a vanishing factor only for the rank-six, 72-root component in the numerical
  row whose component signatures standard root-system classification
  identifies as $E_8\supset E_6+A_2$.

The last statement is a Cartan-restriction theorem. The manuscript's
invariant-theory argument promotes that finite result to the corresponding
ideal-valued radical statement.

The separate $E_6$ incidence checker has a certificate-fed input path:

[`verification/certificates/e6_extrema_certificate.json`](../verification/certificates/e6_extrema_certificate.json) supplies the following witness data:

- the $6\times6$ Cartan matrix and 72 root-coordinate rows;
- six permutations claimed to be the simple reflections;
- a claimed highest-root coordinate row;
- six triple seeds, one for each claimed simultaneous-reflection orbit.

The generated module also records the certificate version and a digest of the
entire JSON file, and derives the index of the first Bourbaki simple root from
the supplied root rows. Expected theorem counts and predicates remain authored
in Lean rather than copied from the JSON. This is independent recomputation
from certificate-fed witnesses, not independent discovery of those witnesses.

[`formal/scripts/generate_lean_data.py`](../formal/scripts/generate_lean_data.py) converts that material into [`CubicAnomaly/E6/Data.lean`](../CubicAnomaly/E6/Data.lean). Expected theorem counts are intentionally absent from the generated module.

Lean then checks that:

- the Cartan matrix is the fixed Bourbaki $E_6$ matrix;
- every reflection permutation agrees coordinate-by-coordinate with the Cartan reflection formula and permutes all 72 roots;
- the roots form the reflection orbit of a simple root, include negatives, and have squared length two, and the supplied highest-root row is the greatest positive root in the simple-root order;
- the three subsystem seeds generate orbits of 27, 36, and 40 masks, each mask is closed under root negation and root addition, and the component root-count/rank signatures are $(40,5)$, $(30,5)+(2,1)$, and $(6,2)^3$; standard simply-laced classification identifies the conventional types $D_5$, $A_5+A_1$, and $A_2^3$;
- their Cartesian product has 38,880 distinct triples;
- six generated simultaneous-reflection orbits are disjoint and cover that product;
- direct intersection calculations give the committed six-cell distribution and both extrema;
- a closed irreducible component with the paired signature $(6,2)$ occurs
  exactly on the two extremal cells; standard simply-laced classification
  identifies it as $A_2$;
- common-subsystem multiplicities, context counts, and labelled containment incidences have the stated values.

The principal theorem for that path is
`CubicAnomaly.E6.finite_certificate_valid`. Named projections expose the
central publication claims.

## Trust model

The project uses `native_decide` because both the exceptional grading census
and reduction over 38,880 triples are practical in compiled native code and
unnecessarily slow in the kernel evaluator. `#print axioms` reports:

```text
propext
Quot.sound
Lean.ofReduceBool
Lean.trustCompiler
```

The first two are Lean core foundational axioms used by Mathlib; the last two
are the `native_decide` bridge from compiled evaluation into a Lean theorem.
CI enumerates all 18 exported theorems, requires this exact receipt for every
one, and requires a complete, total, safe public theorem surface.

Lean, Mathlib, and every Lake dependency are pinned by version or source
revision. CI checksum-verifies the Elan installer, then obtains the selected
Lean toolchain, the `leantar` cache client, and Mathlib build-cache objects
through their standard upstream distribution channels. Those downloaded
compiler, cache-client, and cache objects are not individually hash-locked by
this repository. Accordingly, **L** means accepted
under the disclosed `native_decide`, compiler, and upstream-distribution trust
boundary; it is not a claim of a hermetic binary supply chain. A reviewer who
requires that stronger property should rebuild the locked dependency sources
inside a separately controlled, attestable environment.

On the $E_6$ incidence path, Python translates JSON data into Lean syntax. CI
closes accidental drift by
regenerating that file and requiring byte-for-byte identity. Lean separately
validates the translated coordinates and permutations against the fixed
Cartan formulas. The exceptional census and Cartan cubic selector follow their
own certificate-free route from authored Cartan matrices.

## Complete proof allocation

The manuscript carries the analytic and representation-theoretic spine:

- the structural reduction from the homogeneous hypotheses;
- the compact-simple reduction and exhaustive full-rank/rank-deficient branch arguments;
- the invariant-theory classification of degree-three invariants and the promotion from Cartan restriction to the ideal-valued radical;
- the necessity arguments and exclusions for exceptional and classical rank-deficient cases;
- global integration, central kernels, faithful connected subgroup intersections, and the required branching theorems;
- the final chiral-index calculation.

The exact executable layers discharge the load-bearing finite leaves and carry
the independent finite and arithmetic reconstructions:

- the full-rank exceptional census, Cartan cubic selector, and exceptional
  rank-deficient sieve used by the classification;
- conventional labels for every common root-system type;
- the complete 5,079-subsystem census, Weyl-orbit arithmetic, and kernel calculations.

The complete-matter interpretation **(M)** supplies the declared bridge from
chiral index to physical family count.

## How the finite certificate closes the loop

The JSON records human-readable claims, while Lean's expected theorem counts are authored independently. The checker derives 4,320 by enumerating the reconstructed triple universe, so editing the JSON label `pairwise.maximizers` leaves the theorem statement and computation fixed.

Lean treats the six seeds as witness data, applies all six verified simple reflections, computes each closure, and checks disjointness and coverage of the full Cartesian product.
